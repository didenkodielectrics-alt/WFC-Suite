## Reading the data

- **Deep scan** is the day with the most listings for an item, which is the most trustworthy number. **Latest** is the newest scan day, which was partial: far fewer listings, so only the pricier ones were left. That's why Leaded Vial shows 19g and Winter Squid 85s.
- **Trust** column: *good* means ≥100 listed and the latest price is within 3× of the deep scan. *Deep, but latest swings* means ≥100 listed but the price moved a lot. *Thin* means 20–99 listed. *Very thin* means under 20.
- Anything thin can be set by a single seller. Check Auctionator before you craft or post in bulk.

## Fishing notes

- **Fishing Rack:** its "Camp Benefits" line shows *Fish Bowl* (+8% all stats, Kings-like, doesn't stack with Blessing of Kings) because the Rack carries the Bowl's buff over. Its extras are fishing lures and an hour of **Uncommon Fishing**, which lets you catch *bloated* fish such as Bloated Redgill and Bloated Mud Snapper.
- **Bloated fish are broken or near-broken on live.** Player reports:
  - 4 in 523 catches
  - 5 in about 15 hours
  - 0 in 5 hours in Tanaris

  Blizzard's Nov 10 hotfix notes acknowledge "ongoing issues" with the Rack. Payouts are low-value gems. A failed Rack still starts the shared one-hour camping cooldown. A Fish Bowl gives the same +8% far cheaper.
- **Where to fish at ~192:** Booty Bay and the Stranglethorn coast (zone requirement 130, so nothing escapes). That's where you get Rockscale Cod, Mithril Head Trout (Rack mats), and the Oily Blackmouth and Firefin schools.
  - **Ratchet** is fine and safe for skill-ups, but its water gives lower-value fish.
  - At **205 effective skill** (a +25 lure), move to the Tanaris or Feralas coast for **Stonescale Eel** schools.
- **Oily Blackmouth schools** (Fishing 55; 150 never misses), lowest-level zones first:
  - Barrens coast by Ratchet *(best for Horde)*
  - Darkshore by Auberdine
  - Westfall
  - Hillsbrad's south coast
  - Wetlands, the shipwrecks NW of Menethil
  - Arathi
  - Desolace
  - Dustwallow
  - Swamp of Sorrows
  - Booty Bay docks and the SE Stranglethorn shore *(safe)*
  - Azshara

  Schools despawn after a few catches and respawn elsewhere along the coast, so move along the shore.
- **Bristle Whisker Catfish:** fresh water in level 15–35 zones. Near Crossroads that's Lushwater Oasis, the Stagnant Oasis and the Forgotten Pools, plus Stonetalon lakes and Ashenvale/Hillsbrad rivers. About 86c raw, so it's cooking fodder.
- Zone fishing requirements above are Classic numbers. Forever uses the same per-zone system, but its exact values may differ.

## Craft margins per character

How it's calculated:
- Profit = product price × average quantity × 0.95 (AH cut) − reagent cost.
- It uses deep-scan prices and only counts products with at least 5 listed.
- **Known** means the recipe is in the character's snapshot. **Trainable (est.)** is a trainer recipe whose estimated learn rank is at or below current skill. Confirm those at the trainer.
- Vendor reagents are priced at the lower of AH or an assumed vendor price:
  - Empty Vial 20c, Leaded Vial 80c, Crystal Vial 2s, Simple Wood 38c
  - Coarse Thread 10c, Fine Thread 1s
  - dyes, flux and spices at their usual vendor prices
- A low **Product listed** count means a thin market: craft one, see if it sells.
